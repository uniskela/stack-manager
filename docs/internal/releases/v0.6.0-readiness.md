## Remaining risks

| ID | Risk | Severity | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | A verified push is not routed if its fetch fails all 5 retries (~75 s of backoff, e.g. forge restarting). A later polled fetch does not route it. | Medium | Low–medium | Missed automatic deployment; no unsafe deploy. | Next verified push includes the changes (baseline unchanged); Deploy now; documented. Follow-up [#68](https://github.com/uniskela/stack-manager/issues/68). |
| R2 | Exactly-once delivery is impossible across HTTP; Portainer ignores `Idempotency-Key`. | Medium | Low | Possible duplicate redeploy after a crash/timeout. | Intent before send, unknown outcome, persistent automation hold, operator inspection. |
| R3 | Live forge versions, reverse proxies and receivers may differ from fixtures (byte rewriting, redirects, TLS chains). | Medium | Medium | 401/415 ingress or unknown/rejected sends. | Operator checklist steps 2–3, 5–12 before production opt-in. |
| R4 | Git remote host check happens before `git` resolves DNS (rebinding TOCTOU), already listed in SECURITY.md. | Medium | Low | SSRF to a private address via a hostile DNS name. | HTTPS only, no redirects, private networks denied by default. Deployment webhooks are pinned; Git is not. |
| R5 | In-memory ingress rate limit resets on restart and is skipped without a trustworthy client IP. | Low | Medium | Weak flood protection. | Proxy-level limits (documented). |
| R6 | Restoring an older backup restores an older replay/routing ledger. | Medium | Low | Redeliveries can fetch/route again; sent requests are not undone. | Documented: keep automation off until receivers are inspected. |
| R7 | Delivery, decision and attempt history grows until repository/stack removal. | Low | Medium | Database growth on busy repositories. | Monitor size; retention is future work. |
| R8 | 20 Sonar alerts await manual status changes; 25 reviewed advisories remain from #66. | Low | n/a | Noise in security dashboards. | Dispositions recorded; apply in Sonar. |
| R9 | Dependabot alerts disabled. | Low | Low | Slower notice of new advisories. | `pnpm audit` and Trivy run in CI; consider enabling alerts. |
| R10 | Local Docker build not exercised on the audit host. | Low | Low | — | CI Docker job and container-security pass on `main`. |
| R11 | After R1 occurs, every later successful fetch queues a no-op routing job for that repository. | Low | Low | Negligible worker load. | Covered by the R1 follow-up. |
